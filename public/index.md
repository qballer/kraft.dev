# Kraft Dev: Engineering Kraft in the age of AI agents

Kraft Dev is a monthly, in-person meetup in Tel Aviv for engineers, engineering leaders and product builders. "Kraft" is spelled with a K on purpose.

## What we talk about

1. **Agentic SDLC**: how AI agents change every step of the software lifecycle: planning, coding, review, testing and shipping.
2. **Dark factories**: lights-out software production, where agents build, test and ship with few humans in the loop.
3. **Agents in the product**: shipping agents as product features: architecture, evals, UX, reliability and cost.
4. **Where Kraft still matters**: which hard engineering skills still count, and how we grow engineers when AI writes most of the code.
